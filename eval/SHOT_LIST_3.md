# Recording shot list, round 3: blind test, one hand and two hands

**Purpose:** a fresh test that nobody has looked at. It checks that v3 finds every kind of mistake
on **one-hand** and **two-hand** playing, on the old tunes and on new ones. The takes are labelled
from this list, not by listening. v3 is scored on them **once**, and that score is written down as
the blind result before anyone looks at a single take.

**What you need:** the same piano and phone as before, and about 50 minutes for 49 takes of 10 to
30 seconds each. The optional held-too-long group adds 5 minutes.

**Where the files go:** `recordings/round3/`. Name each file exactly as in the table (`.m4a`), or
record them in table order as "New Recording N" and say so: they can be renamed in order, as in
round 2.

**If you are short on time,** record the takes marked ★ first (20 takes, about 20 minutes): one
take of every mistake type, in both groups.

**If a take goes differently from the plan** (a mistake you did not mean, or a planned mistake you
forgot), don't redo it. Write the take number and what happened in
`recordings/round3/notes.txt`, and the label is fixed from that note.

---

## Before you start

- **Phone:** in the same spot as before, about 1 m from the keyboard. Start recording about a second
  before the first note, and stop about a second after the last one.
- **Tempo:** steady, at your usual speed. A mistake should be the only thing that is different.
- **Late notes:** "wait about one beat" means a clear gap you can hear. "Half a beat" means clearly
  behind, not a slight smudge.
- **Missed notes:** leave the note out but **keep the beat**: the next note still comes on time.
- **Restarts:** stop, go back to the named note, and play on to the end without the mistake.
- Note names give the octave: C4 is middle C.

## The pieces

**Pieces you have played before:** Ode to Joy (melody alone, and melody with the left-hand bass).

**New pieces:**

All pieces are in 4/4: four beats per bar, `|` marks a bar line. The number after each note is how
many beats to hold it:

| Number | Note value | Count |
|---|---|---|
| ½ | eighth note | two notes per beat: "1-and" |
| 1 | quarter note | one beat |
| 2 | half note | two beats |
| 4 | whole note | the whole bar |

**Twinkle** (about 100 beats per minute):

| Hand | Bar 1 | Bar 2 | Bar 3 | Bar 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RH | C4 1, C4 1, G4 1, G4 1 | A4 1, A4 1, G4 2 | F4 1, F4 1, E4 1, E4 1 | D4 1, D4 1, C4 2 |
| LH (two-hand only) | C3 4 | F3 2, C3 2 | F3 2, C3 2 | G3 2, C3 2 |

In the two-hand version, each left-hand note starts together with the right-hand note above it.

**Ode to Joy** (melody alone for one hand; with the bass for two hands):

| Hand | Bar 1 | Bar 2 | Bar 3 | Bar 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RH | E4 1, E4 1, F4 1, G4 1 | G4 1, F4 1, E4 1, D4 1 | C4 1, C4 1, D4 1, E4 1 | E4 1½, D4 ½, D4 2 |
| LH (two-hand only) | C3 2, rest 2 | G3 2, rest 2 | C3 2, rest 2 | G3 2, rest 2 |

**Hot Cross Buns:**

| Bar 1 | Bar 2 | Bar 3 | Bar 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| E4 1, D4 1, C4 2 | E4 1, D4 1, C4 2 | C4 ½ ×4, D4 ½ ×4 | E4 1, D4 1, C4 2 |
